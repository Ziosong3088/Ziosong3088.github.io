Sketchbook 01 — Ink & Wash

Guardian Beast — Ink & Wash

Cheese — Ink & Wash, 2023

Ways to Machu Picchu — Ink, 2026

About

I'm Zio Song, an animation student at Algonquin College in Ottawa, training as a 3D animator. My work mixes Maya animation — lip-sync, posing, timing — with traditional drawing: charcoal life studies, creature sketches, and oil pastel portraits.

  • Autodesk Maya
  • 3D Character Animation
  • Lip-Sync & Acting
  • Animatics & Timing
  • Life Drawing (Charcoal)
  • Oil Pastel
  • Creature & Character Sketching
