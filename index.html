Selected Work

Crouch-Dance Animatic — Maya / 2D Planning

Pose-to-pose animatic study built around anticipation, a deep drop, and a springy bounce-back. Planned frame by frame before touching the rig — the drop and bounce stills above are the two key poses the whole shot hangs on.

Drapery Chair — Charcoal Life Drawing

A side-lit charcoal study of cloth over a chair: big value shapes first, then the fold patterns. Drawing fabric honestly is weight, gravity, and light — the same things that make 3D cloth and posing read well.

Creature Sketch — “Stuck to the Ground”

A creature design sketch in a play-bow pose: head and front paws pressed low, haunches high. Built from animal anatomy reference — the low hock and the spine as a shallow groove are what sell the pose.

“Jackpot!” — Maya Lip-Sync (In Progress)

A 10–15 second lip-sync shot in Maya: a senior character finds exactly what he's been hunting for — “Oh shit, I found what I need… not bad. Jackpot!” Timed frame by frame for cartoony, Tex Avery–style reactions. Showreel clip coming soon.

About
