About Me

Zio Song — 3D Animator & Artist

I'm a visual artist and 3D animator based in Ottawa, Canada, currently in my third year of the Animation program at Algonquin College.

With nearly six years of drawing experience, I have developed a strong foundation in anatomy, form, and visual expression, working primarily with ink and brush alongside oil pastels. I combine this artistic foundation with technical proficiency in 3D software such as Maya and Blender.

I like learning new things, I'm always looking for ways to connect traditional art with digital animation. I especially enjoy making movements, visual storytelling, and creating imaginative worlds and characters.

I believe great animation begins with a strong understanding of art, observation, and movement.

  • Autodesk Maya
  • 3D Character Animation
  • Lip-Sync & Acting
  • Animatics & Timing
  • Life Drawing (Charcoal)
  • Oil Pastel
  • Creature & Character Sketching
